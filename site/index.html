Built around the work

More capable where it matters.

Breeze avoids fake intelligence and decorative controls. Visible features are expected to do real work.

01 · Privacy architecture

Private means ephemeral.

Private sessions stay out of Breeze history, restart recovery, persistent permissions, extension state and download provenance. Tracking parameters are stripped and known tracker requests can be blocked before they leave.

02 · Context

Your browser remembers usefully.

Workspaces, Reading Queue, notes and session snapshots help you return to the thread without turning the browser into another noisy productivity app.

03 · Breeze Flow

Local tools, right where you browse.

Convert images, audio and video locally. Generate hashes and UUIDs. Keep personal files off random upload sites when the job can happen on your own machine.

04 · PDF workspace

Documents deserve a browser that reads.

Search, thumbnails, comfort reading, split, merge, rotate and extract — inside a sandboxed local PDF workspace designed for long-form work.

A calmer browser can still be a powerhouse.

Breeze is being built toward that line: elegant enough for anyone, deep enough for people who live in their browser.
