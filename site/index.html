Built around the work

More capable where it matters.

Breeze avoids decorative controls that pretend to work. Visible features should earn their place.

01 · Privacy architecture

Private means ephemeral.

Private sessions stay out of Breeze history, restart recovery, persistent permissions, extension state and download provenance.

02 · Context

Your browser remembers usefully.

Workspaces, Reading Queue, notes and snapshots help you return to the thread without turning the browser into another noisy productivity app.

03 · Breeze Flow

Local tools, right where you browse.

Convert images, audio and video locally. Generate hashes and UUIDs. Keep personal files off random upload sites.

04 · PDF workspace

Documents deserve a browser that reads.

Search, thumbnails, comfort reading, split, merge, rotate and extract inside a sandboxed local workspace.

A calmer browser can still be a powerhouse.

Breeze is being built toward that line: elegant enough for anyone, deep enough for people who live in their browser.
